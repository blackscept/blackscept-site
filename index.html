Philosophy

Those who understand how things break are best placed to protect them.

Every system fails somewhere. Knowing where, and why, is the difference between hoping something is secure and being able to show it. Security is built on an honest understanding of failure.

Research

Questions come first. Conclusions follow the evidence.

Precision

Defined scope, exact methods, clear and measured reporting.

Responsible application

Offensive knowledge is used with permission, within limits, and in service of defense.

Capabilities

  • Penetration Testing

  • Vulnerability Research

    Close study of software and protocols to find root causes, with findings handled through responsible disclosure.

    Collecting and verifying public information to map exposure, investigate threats and support decisions.

Principles

  • Precision

    Exact scope, exact methods, exact language.

  • Discretion

    Findings and sensitive information are handled with care.

  • Curiosity

    The habit of asking why something works, and what happens when it doesn't.

  • Accountability

    Work is documented, authorised and answerable.

  • Controlled capability

    Skill applied within clear limits and with clear permission.
